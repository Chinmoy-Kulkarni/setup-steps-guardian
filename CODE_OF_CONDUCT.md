# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Assume good intent while reviewing the evidence. Focus
criticism on code, behavior, and documented tradeoffs rather than people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of private
data, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies to repository issues, pull requests, Discussions, reviews, and other spaces
where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock threads, reject contributions, or restrict
participation when necessary to protect the community and project.

For a private conduct report, contact the maintainer through
[GitHub private reporting](https://github.com/Chinmoy-Kulkarni/setup-steps-guardian/security/advisories/new)
and state that the report concerns conduct rather than a software vulnerability. Reports will be
handled as privately as the platform permits. There is no guaranteed response time.

# Roadmap

The roadmap is evidence-driven and may change through public issues and Discussions.

## Now

- Keep the validator aligned with GitHub's documented setup-workflow contract.
- Reduce false positives through public fixtures and human review.
- Publish reproducible ecosystem, adoption, and usefulness measurements without Action telemetry.
- Improve contribution, support, and finding documentation.

## Next

- Add fixtures for Windows, runner groups, self-hosted runners, services, and snapshots.
- Make inferred dependency guidance more configurable without weakening structural validation.
- Publish periodic public-workflow studies and compare changes over time.
- Add verified examples of findings that maintainers fixed successfully.

## Later, only with evidence

- Read-only fleet inventory across selected repositories.
- Organization policy synchronization and drift reporting.
- Optional hosted operation.

The project will not add write permissions, automatic pull requests, broad source retention, or
paid infrastructure merely to expand scope.
